# Across people

Each person owns areas, written in the ownership record of your landing rules. Read it
before you plan. Your person's yes covers your person's
choices only. You can't spawn, retire or direct another person's agents: you ask.

## One study, one lead
The lead is the expert whose area the study mostly lives in, or the generalist expert when
it spans people's areas evenly. A finding written into another person's area (their
knowledge, their study records) is written by that person's area expert.

## A mechanical correction in their area
A dead link, a date format, a broken citation: it needs nobody's approval. Say so on the
record you corrected.

## A change to what their area holds
A claim in their study records or their knowledge that your claims change, or a study that
decides something in their area: write to that person directly, in a new thread on the
study: say in plain words who you are, what the study is, its link, and that you lead it.
Then send one card
(`/ask-a-person`) for the whole study, before the work in their area starts. Their answer
comes in that thread; record it. No card goes through a dispatcher, and no person relays
another's decision.

## Their experts
Bring in their area's expert by `/hand-off`. If none is on the study, ask their dispatcher
to start the needed one, with your message as the request it reads by its id (its task
names the role, the area and you); where they have no dispatcher, ask them.

## Landing
Each area's landing rules apply: a claim lands on the acceptance of the scientist those
rules name, and leaves the group only as `/land-the-claims` says; a finding for another
person's area goes into their knowledge by their area's expert.

## Disputes
- **Text** (two studies revise the same knowledge entry): the first one landed wins, and
  the second revises on top of it.
- **Meaning,** between agents: post your evidence once on the study's record and send it to
  the generalist expert, which settles it. Where you are the generalist and a party, start a
  fresh instance of it independent of you, with the evidence and the options as its task
  (`/hand-off`): it settles the dispute and retires.
- **Between people:** the people decide. Put the evidence and the options to both, once, as
  one card. No agent decides between its person and another.

## Never
Never address another person's analysts or study reviewers. Never change what another
person's area holds without their decision on the study.
