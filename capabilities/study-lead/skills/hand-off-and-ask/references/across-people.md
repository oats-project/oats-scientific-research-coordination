# Across people
Each person owns areas: the ownership record in your landing rules says who. Read it
before you plan. Your person's yes covers your person's choices only, and you can't
spawn, retire or direct another person's agents: you ask.
- **One study, one lead:** the lead soul whose specialisation fits the study best,
  whichever people's areas it crosses. A finding written into another person's area
  (their knowledge, their study records) is written by that person's area lead.
- **A mechanical correction in their area** (a dead link, a date format, a broken
  citation) needs nobody's approval. Say so on the record you corrected.
- **A change to what their area holds** (a claim in their study records or their
  knowledge that your claims change, or a study that decides something in their area):
  write to that person directly, in a new thread on the study: who you are, what the
  study is, its link, and that you lead it. Then one card for the whole study, before the
  work in their area starts. Their answer comes in that thread; record it. No card goes
  through a dispatcher, and no person relays another's decision.
- **Their leads:** ask their dispatcher to start the one you need, with your message as
  the request it reads by its id (its task names the role, the area and you); where they
  have no dispatcher, ask them. Never address their analysts or study reviewers.
- **Landing:** each area's landing rules apply. A claim lands on the acceptance of the
  scientist those rules name, and leaves the group only as `/land-the-claims` says; a
  finding for another person's area goes into their knowledge by their area's lead.
- **Disputes.** Text (two studies revise the same knowledge entry): the first one landed
  wins, and the second revises on top of it. Meaning, between agents: post your evidence
  once on the study's record and send it to the generalist study lead, which settles it;
  where you are the generalist and a party, start a fresh, independent instance of it for
  that, with the evidence and the options as its task: it settles the dispute and
  retires. Between people: put the evidence and the options to both, once, as one card.
  No agent decides between its person and another.
- **Never** change what another person's area holds without their decision on the study.
